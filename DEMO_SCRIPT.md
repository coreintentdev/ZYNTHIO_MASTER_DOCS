## Demo Flow — 3 Minutes

---

### [0:00-0:20] Opening — Credibility First

**Say:**

> "Let me show you what Zynthio actually looks like right now. Not a mockup. Not Figma. Live infrastructure, running on our own server."

**Action:** Open terminal showing Docker containers running. Health checks passing.

**Why this works:** Most early-stage startups show prototypes. You're showing running containers. Credibility is established in the first 20 seconds.

---

### [0:20-0:50] CoreeyAI — The Intelligence Layer

**Say:**

> "This is CoreeyAI — our multi-model AI orchestration layer. It doesn't call one API. It routes tasks to the right model for the job. Claude for reasoning, Grok for real-time data, Perplexity for research, Suno for audio generation."

**Action:** Trigger a live CoreeyAI query — a composition prompt that demonstrates model routing.

**Show:**
- Prompt sent to CoreeyAI
- Response showing which model handled which subtask
- Structured output demonstrating orchestration logic

**Say:**

> "Three AI models, orchestrated in one call. This is what powers SongPal — and what makes gTrade intelligent."

---

### [0:50-1:30] SongPal — The Product

**If SongPal MVP is ready:**

**Say:**

> "This is SongPal — our AI music production platform. Trademark filed in New Zealand."

**Action:** Open SongPal. Demonstrate:
1. Start a new track session
2. Enter a creative prompt ("ambient electronic, 120 BPM, atmospheric pads")
3. Show CoreeyAI processing
4. Play generated audio

**Say:**

> "Original audio, created in seconds, using our own AI layer. The creator owns 100% of it. No licensing traps. No extraction."

**If SongPal is not yet ready (fallback):**

**Say:**

> "SongPal is in active development — trademark filed, beta targeting this quarter. Let me show you the architecture, and play you something the stack produces."

**Action:** Show ECOSYSTEM_MAP.md architecture on GitHub. Walk through the data flow. Play a 15-second clip of *SIGNAL 336* or *THE MIRROR ASKED A QUESTION*.

**Say:**

> "This track was made using the Zynthio stack. DJ Zynrose — my artist project — is the living proof of concept."

---

### [1:30-2:10] CoreIntent / gTrade — The Self-Funding Engine

**Say:**

> "Most early-stage startups burn cash while they build. We built a different mechanism."

**Action:** Show gTrade dashboard or risk config.

**Show:**
- Assets: BTC-PERP, ETH-PERP, SOL-PERP, XAU-PERP, XAG-PERP
- Max leverage: 5.0x | Max risk per trade: 1% | Daily loss ceiling: 0.8%
- Status: live, autonomous, risk-managed

**Say:**

> "This is gTrade. Not a subscription signal service. A competition-based model. Open architecture. Every risk parameter is transparent and verifiable."

**Pause.**

> "This is how we stay sovereign. gTrade funds development. No VC dependency. The bot competes; the results build better tools."

---

### [2:10-2:40] Documentation & Architecture

**Say:**

> "One thing that separates Zynthio from every other early-stage startup I've seen — everything is documented, public, and structured."

**Action:** Open GitHub repo. Scroll through:
- 7 brand directories with README, ROADMAP, ASSETS
- 19 multilingual avatar scripts (6 languages)
- FINANCIAL_MODEL.md, NZ_COMPLIANCE.md

**Say:**

> "Seven brands. Full documentation. Financial projections, compliance tracking, multilingual content — all in one public repo. This is how you build a company, not just a demo."

---

### [2:40-3:00] Close — The Stack Is Real

**Say:**

> "Let me leave you with this."

**Action:** Return to terminal. Services running.

**Say:**

> "What you just saw is a running system. Live infrastructure. Multi-model AI. A trademark on file. A company incorporating in New Zealand this month. An autonomous trading bot funding development. Original music written and ready to deploy. And a founder who built every layer — the code, the brand, the music, and the legal filings."

**Pause. Make eye contact.**

> "Zynthio is sovereign creative infrastructure for the post-AI world. One stack. Seven brands. No filler. All signal."

## Adaptation by Competition Type

### AI competitions
- Extend CoreeyAI to 60 seconds
- Show prompt engineering templates and Perplexity Orb agentic workflow
- Demonstrate model selection logic
- Trim documentation to 15 seconds

### Music competitions
- Open with 10 seconds of *SIGNAL 336*
- Extend SongPal to 60 seconds
- Show MOSOKO curriculum outline
- Trim gTrade to 15 seconds ("how we self-fund")

### Fintech / trading competitions
- Open with gTrade risk config immediately
- Extend gTrade to 90 seconds — live positions, risk params, asset coverage
- Emphasise competition model vs. subscription
- Show FINANCIAL_MODEL.md on screen

### Startup competitions
- Open with market size ($21B+ combined TAM)
- Balance SongPal product and gTrade self-funding
- Show financial projections
- Close with funding ask (NZD $150-250K seed)

### If WiFi fails
- Pre-record a 3-minute screen capture
- Offline screenshots of all key screens
- Music clips stored locally
- Risk config YAML viewable offline
