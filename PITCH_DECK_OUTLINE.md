## Slide 2 — The Problem

### The creator economy is broken. AI trading is gatekept.

**Four pain points:**

1. **AI tools extract value from creators.** Generative music platforms are expensive, opaque, and IP-hostile. Creators produce; platforms profit.

2. **AI trading is a black box behind a paywall.** Retail traders get subscription signal services with no transparency, no accountability, no open architecture.

3. **Education is disconnected from real tools.** Music schools teach legacy workflows. None of them teach the AI tools reshaping the industry.

4. **IP protection is inaccessible.** Independent creators can produce at scale but cannot protect at scale.

**Anchor stat:** Combined addressable market: **$21B+** — and no one offers the full stack.

**Visual:** Four icons representing each pain point. Dark background, minimal.
