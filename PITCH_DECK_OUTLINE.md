## Slide 2 — The Problem

### Creators and traders are fragmented across broken infrastructure.

**Four pain points:**

1. **AI tools are extractive.** Generative music platforms capture value from creators — expensive subscriptions, opaque models, IP-hostile terms. Creators produce; platforms profit.

2. **AI trading is gatekept.** Retail traders get black-box signal subscriptions with no transparency and no accountability. Performance claims are unverifiable by design.

3. **Education is disconnected.** Music programmes teach legacy workflows while AI reshapes the industry. The gap between curriculum and reality grows every quarter.

4. **IP protection is inaccessible.** Trademarks, licensing, and copyright strategy are locked behind expensive gatekeepers. Creators can produce at scale but cannot protect at scale.

**Key stat:** Combined addressable market across music production, AI music, education, and AI-assisted retail trading exceeds **$21 billion** — and no one offers the full stack.

**Visual:** Four icons (lock, broken chain, shield with X, blindfold). Dark background.
