## Slide 2 — The Problem

### Creators and traders are fragmented across broken infrastructure.

**Four pain points:**

1. **AI tools are extractive.** Generative music platforms capture value from creators — expensive subscriptions, opaque models, IP-hostile terms. Creators produce; platforms profit.

2. **AI trading is gatekept.** Algorithmic trading infrastructure is institutional-grade and institutional-priced. Retail traders get black-box signal subscriptions — no transparency, no accountability, no open architecture.

3. **Education is disconnected.** Music schools teach legacy workflows. None teach the AI tools reshaping the industry — because they don't build tools. The gap widens every quarter.

4. **IP protection is inaccessible.** Trademarks, licensing, and copyright strategy are locked behind expensive legal gatekeepers. Creators can produce at scale but cannot protect at scale.

**Key stat:** Combined addressable market exceeds **$21 billion** — and nobody offers the full stack.

**Visual:** Four icons (lock, broken chain, blindfold, shield-X) on dark background.
